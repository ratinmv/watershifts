Research profile

Dr. Nafiseh Salehi Siavashani is a hydrogeologist and researcher whose work is centered on water and the Earth systems that control it.

Watershifts brings together a public-facing collection of research interests, books, articles, scientific media and reading material. The goal is to make hydrogeological ideas easier to discover while keeping the focus on scientific curiosity.
